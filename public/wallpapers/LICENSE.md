# Wallpaper photo credits

These photos ship with the template as scenery wallpapers. Each one was checked on its own source page on the date shown. Only CC0, public-domain, Unsplash License and Pexels License photos are included; credit is given here even where the licence doesn’t require it. `credits.json` is the same list for tools.

Changes to every photo: resized to at most 2560 px wide, converted to WebP, metadata removed, and a 320 px thumbnail made (`<id>-thumb.webp`).

All five are works of US National Park Service employees made as part of their official duties, so they are in the public domain in the United States (tagged PD-USGov-NPS on Wikimedia Commons).
